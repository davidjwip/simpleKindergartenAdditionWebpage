How to Use This Math Game

1. Choose an operation: Click the "+" or "−" button to select addition or subtraction.

2. Select the number limit: Check the "Limit to 20" box to use numbers up to 20, or leave it unchecked for numbers up to 10.

3. Solve the problem: Fill in the missing number in the equation using the number buttons below.

4. Check your answer: The game will automatically check if your answer is correct.

5. Track your progress: See your progress in the progress tracker at the bottom.
