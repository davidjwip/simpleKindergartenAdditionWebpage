# Early arithmetic expectations: England, the United States, Singapore

Addition and subtraction from the end of preschool to the end of Year 2 / Grade 1, for a home drill app used by a four-year-old in Hong Kong (K2). Quotations are verbatim from the primary document cited.

## 1. England

### 1.1 EYFS statutory framework (to age 5)

Early learning goals are the development "children should be expected to have attained by the end of the EYFS" ([EYFS statutory framework, in force 1 September 2026](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a5f8e4fb00f3323bf1a23a1/EYFS_group_and_school_based_from_September_2026.pdf), p. 15). Mathematics has two.

**ELG: Number** — "Children at the expected level of development will: Have a deep understanding of numbers to 10, including the composition of each number. Subitise (recognise quantities without counting) up to 5. Automatically recall (without reference to rhymes, counting or other aids) number bonds up to 5 (including subtraction facts) and some number bonds to 10, including double facts."

**ELG: Numerical Patterns** — "Children at the expected level of development will: Verbally count beyond 20, recognising the pattern of the counting system. Compare quantities up to 10 in different contexts, recognising when one quantity is greater than, less than or the same as the other quantity. Explore and represent patterns within numbers up to 10, including evens and odds, double facts and how quantities can be distributed equally." (same PDF, p. 15; the site's companion Numerical Patterns page 404'd, so the PDF is the citation of record.)

Range: **10**, with a recall obligation only to **5**, plus "some" bonds to 10. Subtraction is not a separate goal: it appears only as "(including subtraction facts)", a bond seen from the other side. Neither ELG mentions `+`, `−` or `=`, nor any duty to write a number sentence. The programme instead asks that children "'have a go' … and not be afraid to make mistakes".

### 1.2 Year 1 (age 5–6)

[Programme of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study); symbols first appear here. Pupils should be taught to

- "read, write and interpret mathematical statements involving addition (+), subtraction (−) and equals (=) signs"
- "represent and use number bonds and related subtraction facts within 20"
- "add and subtract one-digit and two-digit numbers to 20, including 0"
- "solve one-step problems … and missing number problems such as 7 = ? − 9"

The unknown is not confined to the answer slot. Non-statutory notes: "This establishes addition and subtraction as related operations."

### 1.3 Year 2 (age 6–7)

"recall and use addition and subtraction facts to 20 fluently, and derive and use related facts up to 100"; "recognise and use the inverse relationship between addition and subtraction". Preamble: "By the end of year 2, pupils should know the number bonds to 20." No time limit appears.

## 2. United States: CCSS

### 2.1 Kindergarten, K.OA

The heading states the sequence: "Understand addition as putting together and adding to, and understand subtraction as taking apart and taking from." ([CCSS K.OA](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/K/OA/))

- **K.OA.A.1** — "Represent addition and subtraction with objects, fingers, … verbal explanations, expressions, or equations."
- **K.OA.A.2** — "Solve addition and subtraction word problems, and add and subtract within 10, e.g., by using objects or drawings …"
- **K.OA.A.3** — "Decompose numbers less than or equal to 10 into pairs in more than one way … (e.g., 5 = 2 + 3 and 5 = 4 + 1)."
- **K.OA.A.4** — "For any number from 1 to 9, find the number that makes 10 when added to the given number …"
- **K.OA.A.5** — "Fluently add and subtract within 5."

Symbols are one option in an *or* list; ceiling within 10; fluency only within 5.

### 2.2 Grade 1, 1.OA

- **1.OA.A.1** — "Use addition and subtraction within 20 to solve word problems … with unknowns in all positions."
- **1.OA.B.4** — "Understand subtraction as an unknown-addend problem. For example, subtract 10 - 8 by finding the number that makes 10 when added to 8."
- **1.OA.C.6** — "Add and subtract within 20, demonstrating fluency for addition and subtraction within 10."
- **1.OA.D.8** — "Determine the unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation relating three whole numbers. For example … 8 + ? = 11, 5 = \_ - 3, 6 + 6 = \_."

([CCSS 1.OA](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/1/OA/)) Subtraction is re-derived from addition, and the missing-operand equation is a Grade 1 objective; no time limit is set.

## 3. Singapore

### 3.1 Kindergarten K1–K2 (ages 4–6): Nurturing Early Learners

NEL covers "children aged four to six (i.e., Nursery 2, Kindergarten 1 and Kindergarten 2)" ([NEL 2022: Educators' Guide for Numeracy](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/57/c079b912-2898-42e2-b32d-ff3ab7cfbec4/Nurturing%20Early%20Learners%202022%20Educators%20Guide%20Numeracy_new.pdf), MOE). The number strand, verbatim:

"LEARNING GOAL 3 Develop counting skills and number sense
3.1 Rote count to at least 20
3.2 Count reliably to at least 10 things
3.6 Write numbers in numerals
3.7 Compare the quantities of two sets of up to 10 things each and use 'same as', 'more than', 'fewer than' and 'less than' appropriately
3.8 Name the parts that form the whole in a quantity of up to 10 (e.g., 5 is made up of 2 and 3, and 1 and 4)"

**No addition or subtraction objective appears**, and no operator symbol. Part-whole understanding "lays the foundation for understanding operations such as addition where they understand formal operations, e.g., 6 = 2 + 4 or 6 = 5 + 1, which they will encounter in primary school" (Chapter 1).

### 3.2 Primary 1 (age 6–7)

P1 content, verbatim from the 2021 syllabus updated October 2025 ([Mathematics Syllabus Primary One to Six](https://www.moe.gov.sg/api/media/92bff26d-b2b4-4535-b868-b8415c744b91/2021-Primary-Mathematics-Syllabus-P1-to-P6-Updated-October-2025.pdf), p. 31): "1. Numbers up to 100 … 2. Addition and Subtraction 2.1 concepts of addition and subtraction 2.2 use of +, – and = 2.3 relationship between addition and subtraction 2.4 adding more than two 1-digit numbers 2.5 adding and subtracting within 100 2.6 adding and subtracting using algorithms 2.7 mental calculation involving addition and subtraction • within 20".

Operators arrive as an objective in their own right ("2.2 use of"), both operations linked (2.1, 2.3) before algorithms (2.6). Written ceiling **100**, mental ceiling **20**.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| England EYFS (Reception) | 4–5 | Bonds and "subtraction facts"; composition | to 10; recall bonds to 5 | None: no `+`, `−`, `=` in either ELG |
| England Year 1 | 5–6 | Add/subtract; missing numbers incl. `7 = ? − 9` | to 20, incl. 0 | Yes: `+`, `−`, `=` read and written |
| England Year 2 | 6–7 | Facts fluently; inverse relationship | to 20 fluently, derive to 100 | Yes; column recording (non-statutory) |
| US CCSS Kindergarten | 5–6 | Put together / take apart; decompose; make 10 | within 10; fluent within 5 | Optional: "expressions, or equations" |
| US CCSS Grade 1 | 6–7 | Add/subtract, unknowns in all positions | within 20; fluent within 10 | Yes: symbol for the unknown |
| Singapore NEL K1–K2 | 4–6 | None; part-whole only (5 is 2 and 3) | to 10 | Numerals only (3.6); no operators |
| Singapore P1 | 6–7 | Concepts, algorithms, mental work | written 100; mental 20 | Yes: "use of +, – and =" (2.2) |

## What a four-year-old drill app may and may not assume

**May assume:** bonds within 5 and parts-of-a-whole within 10 (EYFS "number bonds up to 5"; NEL 3.8; K.OA.A.3); instant recognition to 5 and verbal "more/fewer/same" (EYFS Number and Numerical Patterns; NEL 3.7); tapping a recognised numeral, since NEL 3.6 requires *writing* numerals.

**May not assume**

- **Reading `+`, `−`, `=` as a precondition.** No EYFS ELG contains an operator; Singapore introduces "use of +, – and =" at P1 and puts `6 = 2 + 4` in primary school. A symbolic equation on screen runs ahead of all three preschool curricula: enrichment, not a gap.
- **A blank operand equal to a blank answer.** "Unknowns in all positions" is Grade 1 (1.OA.A.1, 1.OA.D.8); the kindergarten analogue is narrow (make 10, K.OA.A.4). Blanking `a` or `b` is harder.
- **Sums to 20 as default.** Every preschool source stops at 10 (EYFS ELG; K.OA.A.2; NEL 3.7/3.8); 20 is Year 1 / Grade 1 / P1. The 5–20 limit spans three school years: 5 preschool, 10 Reception, 20 Year 1.
- **Timed fluency.** No document sets a time limit or rate; the strongest words are "automatically recall" (EYFS, bonds to 5) and "fluently" (K.OA.A.5 within 5; 1.OA.C.6 within 10; Year 2 to 20). Automaticity is a memory claim, not a speed test.
- **That a wrong answer needs no second attempt.** The EYFS programme asks that children "have a go" and "are not afraid to make mistakes"; single-tap, no-retry submission has no support here.
